Transformer health check

Estimates a power transformer's condition and remaining life from its dissolved gas readings (H2, CO, C2H4, C2H2).

Input: a CSV file with 420 rows (one reading every 12 hours, about 210 days) and the columns H2, CO, C2H4, C2H2.

Waking up the models… The server is loading the two AI models. This can take a minute or two after a period of inactivity.
